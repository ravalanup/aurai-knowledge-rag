# Holiday Season Staffing Forecast

Demand modelling shows the park needs 340 additional seasonal staff for the holiday season, concentrated in
the weeks around the winter break.

## Status
Hiring is 85% complete. The largest gap is in food service, where 48 positions are still open. Guest
Relations and ride operations are fully staffed.

## Actions
Offer part-time shifts to existing staff first, and run a second hiring event in the first week of December.
