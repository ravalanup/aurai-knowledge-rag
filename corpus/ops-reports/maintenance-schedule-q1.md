# Planned Maintenance Schedule, Q1

## Attraction closures
The Comet Coaster is closed from January 12 to January 16 for its annual track inspection. The Skyline
Wheel is closed on February 3 for a main bearing replacement. The Harbor Log Flume is drained for pump
overhaul during the first week of March.

## Notes
Closures are scheduled on the lowest-attendance weekdays. Guest communications go out two weeks before each
closure.
