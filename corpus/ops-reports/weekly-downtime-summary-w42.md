# Weekly Ride Downtime Summary, Week 42

Overall ride availability this week was 97.4%.

## Unplanned downtime by attraction
The Comet Coaster had the most unplanned downtime at 3.2 hours in total, caused by a faulty hydraulic
pressure sensor that was replaced on Wednesday. The Harbor Log Flume lost 1.1 hours to a pump trip. All other
attractions were below 30 minutes.

## Follow-up
Spare hydraulic sensors have been added to the Comet Coaster parts kit.
