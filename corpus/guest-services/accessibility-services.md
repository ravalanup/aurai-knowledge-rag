# Accessibility Services

## Accessibility Access Pass
Guests who cannot wait in a standard queue can request an Accessibility Access Pass at Guest Relations
inside the main gate. A pass covers the guest and up to 4 companions for the day and does not need medical
paperwork.

## Mobility and comfort
Wheelchairs and electric scooters can be rented at the main gate for $15 per day. Quiet rooms for guests who
need a break from noise and crowds are available at Harbor Plaza and near the Skyline Wheel.
