# Guest Service Recovery Playbook

## Goodwill credits
Guest Relations team members may approve a goodwill credit of up to $75 without asking a manager. Credits
from $75 to $300 need shift lead approval. Anything above $300 goes to the duty manager.

## Recording the outcome
Every credit is logged in the guest case record with the reason and the approver. Do not promise a refund
before the case is logged.
