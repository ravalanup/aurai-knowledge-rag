# Ticket Refund Policy

## Standard refunds
Tickets can be refunded in full up to 7 days before the visit date. Refunds go back to the original form of
payment and arrive within 10 business days.

## Late changes
Within 7 days of the visit date a ticket is not refunded. It is converted to a credit that stays valid for
12 months and can be used for any single-day ticket.

## Weather and closures
If the park closes for the whole day because of severe weather, guests can rebook for any date in the next
90 days at no charge.
