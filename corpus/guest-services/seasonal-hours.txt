Seasonal operating hours

Winter (November to February): the park opens at 10am and closes at 8pm. The evening lantern walk runs
until 8pm.

Summer (June to August): the park opens at 9am and closes at 10pm. The fireworks show starts at 9:30pm.

Spring and autumn follow a 9am to 9pm schedule. Holiday dates can differ, so check the app on the day of
your visit.
